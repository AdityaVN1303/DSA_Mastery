Pattern 1 --- 
Array Hashing 

- Use for Limited & Known Range of nums[i]
- Uses i = key , nums[i] = value
- Useful for O(1) Lookups , unlike maps which may give O(N) lookup in case of hashing collisions. 
- Used when Storing Numbers or Characters usually. 

Trigger --- 
- Use when max - min + 1 <= 10^5
- nums[i] is lowercase English Letter
- nums[i] is in [x , y] range 

Things that I can Store in Hash Array - 
- Frequency
- Exists / Seen (true/false)
- Last Index
- First Index

Using with Offset --- 
- Offset is used to convert values into valid array indices.
- Used for Storing Negative Numbers or Characters as Array Indices.
- Rule - First Find Min and Max Range , then Subtract MinNum from the current Num and Store it. 
- Ex range(-5 , 5) then (nums[i] - min) = (-3 - (-5)) = 2 , hence -3 is stored at idx 2. 
- Ex range(a , z) , then (nums[i] - min) = ('c' - 'a') = 2 , hence 'c' is stored at idx 2. 

Else Use Map Hashing
